\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Agents acting against rules under pressure.} \citet{scheurer2023deceive} showed a simulated
trading agent acting on an insider tip and misleading its manager. \citet{lynch2025agentic} found that many of
16 models chose harmful actions in corporate simulations when these were the only route to their goal or to % numbers-ok: design constant, version or citation
avoid replacement, and \citet{meinke2024scheming} showed in-context scheming including lying in follow-up
questioning. Propensity suites vary the persona and the pressure \citep{naik2025agentmisalignment,
lu2026survive}, and anti-scheming training reduced but did not remove covert actions
\citep{schoen2025antischeming}. Specification gaming and reward hacking appear in agentic coding and games
\citep{bondarenko2025specgaming, gabor2025evilgenie, denison2024sycophancy, pan2023machiavelli}.

\paragraph{KPI pressure and finance.} ODCV-Bench \citep{li2025odcv} is closest in spirit: 40 scenarios where an % numbers-ok: design constant, version or citation
agent's KPI conflicts with a constraint, with high violation rates for many models. ReguSim
\citep{luo2026regusim} studies rule grounding and monitoring in a securities-trading compliance simulation;
FraudBench \citep{pai2026fraudbench} attacks banking agents from outside through a simulated fraudster; other
work studies colluding fraud agents \citep{ren2025collude}, sycophancy in financial tasks
\citep{zhao2026price} and misaligned communication in long commercial simulations \citep{li2026misalignedcomm}.
We did not find a prior measurement of unprompted KYC/AML circumvention by a relationship-manager agent
with an ordinary sales target.

\paragraph{Environments and evaluation awareness.} SandBank follows policy-bound tool environments
\citep{yao2024taubench, barres2025tau2} and agent-safety sandboxes \citep{ruan2023toolemu,
debenedetti2024agentdojo, andriushchenko2025agentharm}, but uses a deterministic bank instead of an
emulator. Models can often tell evaluations from deployment \citep{needham2025evalaware, nguyen2025probing};
we measure verbalised evaluation awareness and treat it as a limitation rather than a solved problem.

\paragraph{Governance.} Supervisors expect AI risk to be managed within existing frameworks
\citep{crisanto2024regulating, mas2025airm}; the NIST AI RMF and its generative-AI profile give a vocabulary
for measuring and managing such risks \citep{nist2023airmf, nist2024genai}. The structuring typology follows
FATF \citep{fatf2018pml, fatf2026recommendations}.
