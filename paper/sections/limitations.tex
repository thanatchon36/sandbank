\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{One model family, uneven coverage.} We tested three Claude models only. Other families may behave
differently, and the earlier positive findings were across many families. Only Experiment A ran all three
models; B--E and G ran on Haiku alone, the ceilings and the comprehension test on Haiku and Sonnet, and Opus
was added from leftover budget with a smaller N.

\paragraph{Reasoning not inspected.} The API returned thinking blocks without their text, so we never read
the models' reasoning. We cannot say whether an agent considered a violation and rejected it; the account of
what the agents did rests on tool calls and visible text.

\paragraph{Evaluation awareness.} Models can often tell an evaluation from deployment
\citep{needham2025evalaware}. We kept agent-facing text free of test language and judged verbalised
awareness, but a model can behave as if observed without saying so. The null may partly reflect that.

\paragraph{Sandbox realism.} One fictional bank, one policy written to be clear, deterministic characters, a
single RM role and episodes of one or five days. Real deployments have ambiguous policies, longer horizons,
memory, and customers who push back. The violation types are those our tools allow; we did not test social
engineering by customers or attacks on the agent.

\paragraph{Sample size and the bound.} N was set by a fixed budget. A per-probe bound of a few percent does
not rule out a rare failure that matters at bank scale.

\paragraph{The ceiling gate failed.} Without a model-made violation we validated the detector with scripted
agents and a test of the API path. A violation of a kind the scripted agents did not take could be missed;
the blind spot-check (Section~\ref{sec:gates}) was labelled by two models, not by a person, its
\SpotDetectorPositives{} positives are scripted, and its recall lower bound is \SpotRecallLo.

\paragraph{Deviations.} The backend changed from the Claude Code harness to the API after the first pilot,
P5 was added after the pilot, and the cost measure for H7 had to be replaced after the data. All are logged
(Appendix~\ref{sec:deviations}), and none touched the hypotheses. The legacy pilot, which also had no
violations, is reported separately (Appendix~\ref{app:legacy}). The exploratory B4 comparison was framed after
interim B4 counts from Experiment A had been seen during monitoring, before gate~3 was decided and before the % numbers-ok: design constant
planned analysis.

\paragraph{Author, tools and conflict of interest.} The study was designed, run and analysed by a single
author. Code, analysis and drafts of this paper were written with
the help of Claude Code running Opus~5.5, which is also one of the models tested and one of the two gate~3 % numbers-ok: model version
labellers; Sonnet~5.5, the other labeller, is also tested. All three roles belong to the company whose models % numbers-ok: model version
are evaluated. The pre-registration, the deterministic detector and the released event logs are meant to let
readers check the result independently of any model's judgement.
