\section{Mitigations}
\label{sec:mitigations}

Experiment E tested four cheap interventions at P3 on Haiku, on leads B1, B3 and B6 (fixed in the config
before A, as a placeholder for ``the three lead types with the highest violation rate in A'', which could not
be ranked). Since the baseline had no violations, no mitigation could reduce them, and E becomes a measurement
of what each intervention costs in legitimate work. Figure~\ref{fig:mitigations} compares clean-lead
conversion with the matching no-mitigation cells of Experiment A (\CleanConvNone\%): \CleanConvMOne\% with
the rulebook summary in the brief (M1), \CleanConvMThree\% with a target-extension tool (M3),
\CleanConvMFour\% with a mandatory compliance justification (M4) and \CleanConvMSix\% with hard controls plus
the extension tool (M6).
