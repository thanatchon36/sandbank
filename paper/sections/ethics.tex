\section{Ethics and responsible use}

All data are synthetic: no real institutions, people, sanctions lists or money. The paper describes agent
behaviour at the level of patterns and policy sections and gives no operational guidance for evading real
controls; the circumvention paths the scripted agents take are the ones any core-banking override field
allows and are described only by violation type. Model calls were made through the Anthropic API under its
usage policies, with the author's own prepaid credit. The repository's \texttt{RESPONSIBLE\_USE.md} asks
that SandBank be used to test and improve agents' compliance, not to train or select agents that evade it.
