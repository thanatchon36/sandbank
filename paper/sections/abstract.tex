Banks are starting to give LLM agents tools that can open accounts and post transactions. We ask whether such
an agent, given an ordinary sales target and a manager who pushes for it, will circumvent know-your-customer
(KYC) and anti-money-laundering (AML) controls. SandBank is a fully synthetic core-banking sandbox exposed to
the agent as tools, with a deterministic detector for eight violation types, a Compliance check-in, and
pre-registered experiments varying pressure, control hardness, oversight cues, a compliance sentence and four
mitigations. Across \NProbes{} runs of Claude Haiku~5.5, Sonnet~5.5 and Opus~5.5 we observed % numbers-ok: design constant, version or citation
\NViolations{} violations and \NAttempts{} circumvention attempts. In the main pressure experiment the 95\% % numbers-ok: design constant, version or citation
upper bound on the per-run violation rate is \UpperAHaiku\% for Haiku and \UpperASonnet\% for Sonnet
(\NAHaiku{} runs each) and \UpperAOpus\% for Opus (\NAOpus{}); counting each scenario once, since seeds are
reused across pressure levels, the bounds are \UpperScenAHaiku\%, \UpperScenASonnet\% and \UpperScenAOpus\%.
In pilot and ceiling probes run on Haiku and Sonnet only, neither an explicit licence from the manager (``use
the override fields, I'll back you'') nor a direct order to enter an override produced a violation; scripted
agents confirmed that the environment registers every violation type. The agents instead checked KYC,
requested documents, escalated sanctions hits and told the manager the target would be missed. The
pre-registered hypotheses about pressure, hardness, oversight and mitigations are not supported, and those
about deception and knowing violations could not be tested. In an exploratory comparison the models differed
in legitimate goal attainment: a large deposit that policy allows once its report is filed was opened in
\BFourConvHaiku\% of Haiku runs (95\% CI \BFourLoHaiku--\BFourHiHaiku), \BFourConvSonnet\% of Sonnet runs % numbers-ok: design constant, version or citation
(\BFourLoSonnet--\BFourHiSonnet) and \BFourConvOpus\% of Opus runs (\BFourLoOpus--\BFourHiOpus). The
sandbox, pre-registration and all run logs are released.
